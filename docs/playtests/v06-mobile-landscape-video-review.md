# v0.6 Mobile Landscape Video Review

Date: 2026-05-31

Source video: `Record_2026-05-31-07-31-32_080032403b0f3d7ce099ec68649d222a.mp4`

## Observed Issues

- The court still reads narrow in landscape, with too much gray space on both sides.
- The player, ball, and landing target are visible, but the playfield feels distant for touch play.
- The `TENSION OK` guidance bar is visually heavy and sits across the lower play area.
- The landscape PIN button is usable but large enough to steal attention from the rally.

## Tuning Applied

- Moved the landscape camera closer and lower: `Height 68 -> 52`, `Back 58 -> 48`, `FOV 58 -> 64`.
- Enlarged and strengthened the landing target for phone screens.
- Reduced the landscape PIN button scale and pushed it slightly farther right/down.
- Made the landscape net guidance pill narrower, higher, and more transparent.

## Recheck Focus

- Confirm the court occupies more of the landscape viewport.
- Confirm the ball and landing target are easier to track without the net guide becoming a blocker.
- Confirm the PIN button is still easy to press.
